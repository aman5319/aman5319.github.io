      💊 Suggested Supplementation Guide

      Simple Supplementation Recommendations

      • Vitamin D: D3 drops/capsules
      • Calcium: Calcium + Vitamin D3
      • Iron: Ferrous Salts
      • Vitamin B12: Methylcobalamin
      • Omega-3: Fish Oil or Algal Oil
      • Magnesium: Magnesium Glycinate/Citrate
      • Zinc: Zinc Picolinate/Gluconate
      • Protein: Whey/Casein/Plant Powder
      • Fibre: Psyllium Husk
      • B-Complex: Multivitamin/B-Complex
      • Vitamin C: Ascorbic Acid
      • Iodine: Kelp or Iodide
